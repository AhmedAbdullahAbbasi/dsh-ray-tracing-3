# Smooth launch sampler: NewDust rerun, 2026-10-09

All 14 final cases pass the fixed absolute-normalization, radial, sampling, transport, seed-consistency and azimuthal gates. This is a first-order, time-integrated, low-optical-depth regression; individual faint pixels and higher scattering orders are not established as converged.

## Change and provenance

Base: `development/physics-improvements` at `a1cb0586bda367deacc89da5ee14ecc0070262c8`. The source bytes and frozen material files were matched to the remote Git blobs before editing. The local reconstructed snapshot has a separate root commit SHA recorded in each provenance file; it is not the remote base SHA.

The production CLI and simulation driver now default to a smooth circular tangent-plane proposal. Its 2-D radial component has density s²/[π(s²+r²)²], with energy-dependent scales chosen from phase medians and column-weighted cloud distances. A 5% uniform forward-hemisphere component supplies full support and bounds each isotropic importance factor by 10. Every packet uses the full normalized mixture PDF per steradian, including the exact Jacobian. The physical source fluence, transport kernel, cloud geometry, scattering tables and observer scorer are unchanged.

The old rectangular production proposal and nested validation proposals remain selectable. FITS and NPZ include sampler labels, the proposal energy grid, scales, component probabilities and defensive probability. Coverage rectangles are explicitly metadata rather than radial-proposal cutoffs.

## Matched physical setup

D=11.5 kpc; Φ=1 ph cm⁻²; total NH=8×10²⁰ cm⁻²; energies 3.3, 4.9 and 6.9 keV; cloud fractions f=d/D=0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 from the observer. DSH screens are 1 pc thick, uniform transversely, with explicit vacuum gaps for multiple clouds. Multiple-cloud NH is divided equally among the three distances. Absorption is disabled; all arrival times are included. Raw pixels are 4 arcsec in a ±600-arcsec field, and previews sum to 20-arcsec display pixels. Annuli are 20 arcsec except the narrow f=0.75, 6.9-keV case, which uses 10 arcsec.

NewDust/xdust 1.0 is evaluated independently using `ScreenGalHalo.norm_int`, x_ND=1−f, the identical grain model and columns, and finite-pixel integration. Its normalized intensity already includes column and geometry; only Φ multiplies it. There is no fitted amplitude or unit-sum normalization. FITS stores ph cm⁻² per pixel; radial profiles store ph cm⁻² arcsec⁻².

## Gates and limits

The aperture gate uses 3 MC standard errors plus the recorded optically-thin versus extinguished comparison allowance, finite-thickness and geometry heuristics, and reference numerical error. Resolved annuli use 4 standard errors plus the same allowances. At least ten annuli must have Neff≥100, aperture relative MC error must be ≤3%, reference refinement error ≤0.3%, and the three-seed consistency p-value ≥0.001. Numerical terminal states and first-order image coverage must be clean. These thickness/geometry allowances are regression heuristics, not a rigorous proof of convergence.

New azimuthal checks use identical finite-pixel masks for both models and a joint m=2/m=4 cosine/sine statistic with covariance from weighted first-order history moments. A radial window must have Neff≥300, every one of its 16 sectors Neff≥15, and full-rank covariance. At least three windows must resolve; every resolved window requires p≥0.001. This detects excess elliptical/fourfold mean structure. It does not prove that every pixel has adequate statistics. The radial proposal itself has no angular box boundaries, so its variance no longer jumps at those boundaries.

At matched 3-million-history counts in the 3.3-keV f=0.5 pilot, aperture relative MC error decreases from 1.0697% with the previous nested proposal to 0.5963% with the radial proposal. The shared-scale before/after image shows a circular centre; faint outskirts retain pixel noise. The high-energy narrow cases remain particularly sparse outside their resolved windows.

The frozen differential law agrees directly with NewDust at each tested energy (reported maximum relative error zero). Its solid-angle integral is about 1.04463 times NewDust’s separately reported RG/Drude total coefficient. This existing discrepancy is recorded and preserved; neither reference nor simulation is rescaled to hide it.

There are zero consistency issues. All 17 halo/radial and azimuthal plots were visually inspected. Fourteen records are final cases, two are duplicate pilot/release checks, and one is the retained sampling failure.
